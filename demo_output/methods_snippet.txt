Spectroscopic and electronic structure simulations for Platinum(II) Tetraphenylporphyrin / Pt(111) Surface were performed with ORCA 6.0 & VASP 6.4 at the wB97X-D / def2-TZVP level of theory. Spectral convolution, empirical scaling, and electronic structure audits were certified using SpecCert v1.0.0 (Monreal-Hernández, 2026). Time-dependent density functional theory (TD-DFT) was used to compute vertical electronic excitations (6 states), yielding an absorption maximum of lambda_max = 418.4 nm with maximum oscillator strength f = 1.2500. Harmonic vibrational frequencies were scaled by an empirical factor of 0.9570 (wB97X-D) to account for anharmonicity and basis set limitations, convoluted with a Lorentzian profile (FWHM = 12 cm^-1). Electronic density of states (DOS) and d-band parameters were evaluated following Hammer-Norskov theory, yielding a filled d-band center of eps_d = -2.463 eV (relative to E_F) with width W_d = 1.315 eV. The computational spectroscopy audit achieved an overall quality status of: PASS.
